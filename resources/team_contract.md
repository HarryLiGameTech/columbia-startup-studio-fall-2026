# Columbia Startup Studio: Team Contract

*Draft for classroom use. Not legal advice. No lawyer has reviewed it.*



**Team name:** ______________________  **Semester:** Fall 2026

### 1. Showing up for each other

Each of us agrees to:

- Communicate with the team. Answer messages, say when we are stuck, and flag early if we cannot deliver.
- Contribute real work every week and do what we said we would do.
- Treat each other with respect.

We understand that our teammates have a hand in our grade. Each of us will complete peer evaluations, and those evaluations affect individual grades.

### 2. Who owns the work

- **No one owns the project alone.** Everything we make together this semester belongs to all of us.
- **Anyone can carry it forward.** After the semester, any member may keep working on the ideas and the work we made together, alone or with others.
- **If we reach an impasse, the joint project shuts down.** No one can hold the project hostage or stop another member from continuing with the ideas on their own.
- **No one owes anyone money or equity** because of this class project, unless we later sign a separate agreement.

### 3. Prior work

- Each of us keeps ownership of anything we built, wrote, designed, or invented before joining this team ("Prior Work").
- Each of us lists our Prior Work on the attached Prior Work list. A blank or "None" list means we have nothing to exclude.
- If a member brings Prior Work into the project, they give the team a free license to use it in the project. The member still owns it. They cannot pull it back out of the version already in use.
- A member who continues the project after the semester needs the owner's permission to keep using someone else's Prior Work.

## Starter Working Agreement (not part of the contract)

A starting point so your team does not begin from zero. Edit it freely. It is not binding and you do not sign it.

**Roles** (suggested; rotate them if you like)

- **Lead / coordinator:** keeps the weekly plan and runs meetings.
- **Customer lead:** books and tracks customer interviews.
- **Builder:** owns the prototype or product.
- **Storyteller:** owns decks, demos, and write-ups.
- **Note-taker:** records decisions and who does what.

**Meetings**

- One team meeting per week, same time each week.
- Each meeting ends with a list of who does what by when.
- Can't make it? Tell the team before the meeting, not after.

**Communication**

- One shared channel (Slack, WhatsApp, Discord, or similar) for all team talk.
- Reply within 24 hours on weekdays.
- Shared files live in one shared folder everyone can reach.
- Raise problems early and directly with the person, then with the team, then with the instructor.
