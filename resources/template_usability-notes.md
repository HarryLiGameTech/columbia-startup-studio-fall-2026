# Usability Testing Notes Template

Use this for Homework 7. Run 3 to 5 sessions with real people in your target audience.

## Reminders

Usability testing differs from our earlier user interviews, in that you are now focusing on how people use your product and improving the products ease of use.

**Behavior is more important than opinion.** Write down what people did, not how you think they felt.

| Not this (opinion) | This (behavior) |
|---|---|
| She found it confusing. | She clicked the X three times. |
| They liked the concept. | He said "Oh wow!" when reading the second onboarding screen. |

**What to look for:**

- **Hesitations:** a pause before clicking
- **Wrong clicks:** they tried the wrong path first
- **Questions out loud:** "Wait, so this is...?"
- **Dropped tasks:** they gave up
- **Workarounds:** they got there an unexpected way

Your recording is the backup. Rewatch it when you write the synthesis. You'll catch things you missed live.
