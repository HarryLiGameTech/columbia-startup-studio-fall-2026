# Usability Session Consent Form Template

## Note to students

Use this form before every usability session you record. Fill in the bracketed parts, then give it to your participant to read and sign. A printed copy, a PDF, or a Google Form all work.

If someone says no to recording, you can still run the session.

## How we'll use what we learn

- We won't use your name in anything we share. We'll call you "Participant 1".

## Your choices

- Taking part is voluntary.
- You can skip any task or question.
- You can stop at any time, for any reason. Just say so.
- If you stop early, you still get the thank-you.
- You can ask us to delete your recording later. Email the contact above.
