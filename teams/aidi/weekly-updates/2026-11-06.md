# Weekly Update - 2026-11-06

## Accountability

Last week, we:

- Added a registration system so that user profiles, preferences, and progress can be saved over time.
- Added My College List as the foundation for expanding the product from college discovery into early application planning.
- Expanded the dataset to 50 colleges, giving users broader search coverage and access to more institutional information.

These changes establish the persistent user experience and product foundation needed for launch. They also allow users to return to their saved information and continue their college-planning process over multiple sessions.

## Planning

This week, we plan to:

- Launch the product and onboard the users who have already committed to testing it.
- Add an application task checklist to My College List so users can move from selecting colleges to preparing their applications.
- Expand the dataset from 50 to 100 colleges.
- Address the issues identified during usability testing, including unclear save and next-step states, incomplete Chinese localization, unexplained specialized terminology, difficult navigation on institution detail pages, and unclear post-waitlist messaging.

These priorities will make the product usable by real users after launch while expanding both its college coverage and application-planning capabilities.
