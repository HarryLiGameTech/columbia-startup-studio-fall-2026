## Overview

This folder contains the research synthesis, audience models, brand foundation, visual system, and product-repository setup developed for HW 4. Together, these materials provide shared context for building **Aplovia**, a bilingual college-application guidance platform for international high-school students applying for first-year admission to U.S. undergraduate programs.

## Results

The team combined its earlier personalized school-list and school-profile concepts into one product direction. The Phase 1 MVP focuses on helping international students understand source-backed reach, match, and safety classifications, build a financially realistic college list, and manage application steps and deadlines while retaining control over every final decision.

The work established three distinct user perspectives, a trust-centered brand position, the Aplovia product name and logo, a professional Midnight Navy interface system, and a private product repository prepared for AI-assisted development.
