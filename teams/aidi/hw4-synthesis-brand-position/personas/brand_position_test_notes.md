# Persona Reactions to the Aplovia Brand Position

## Brand position tested

The three persona agents reviewed the complete `brand_position.md` after the first-round concerns had been incorporated.

## Persona notes

### Aarav Sharma — unsupported first-time applicant

**Reaction: approve, 9/10.** Aarav said the revised position now provides a trustworthy roadmap and treats affordability as a separate, essential part of deciding whether a school is possible. He approved the separation of admission difficulty, affordability, and personal fit, while suggesting that future product copy should make privacy, pricing, and prioritized next steps equally visible.

### Mina Park — preference-uncertain school-list builder

**Reaction: approve, 9/10.** Mina said the revised position clearly promises preference-based discovery, transparent evidence, separate fit and affordability reasoning, and honest uncertainty instead of another ranking list. She suggested showing how changing a preference changes the recommendations and presenting meaningful drawbacks as well as strengths; both principles were added to the brand values.

### Daniel Kim — multi-application process manager

**Reaction: approve, 8/10.** Daniel said the revised position now sounds capable of replacing part of his spreadsheet-and-portal workflow rather than becoming another generic admissions guide. He highlighted preservation of existing list details, prioritized deadlines, official sources, and visible requirement changes; these points were incorporated into the objection-handling response.

## Distinctness check

All three personas approved the final document, but for different reasons. Aarav judged financial feasibility and access to guidance, Mina judged preference discovery and matching transparency, and Daniel judged workflow replacement and deadline reliability. Their distinct priorities remained visible after revision, indicating that they represent separate audience needs rather than cosmetic variations of one user.
