# CallIt: demand strategy

CallIt helps private friend groups keep a shared record of predictions and results. This plan focuses on organic outreach and early feedback.

## Traffic ownership

| Owner | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Shiyu Zhou (Emily) | Weekly traffic review, community participation, posts, and feedback. |
| Aviv Pinto | Social content, group outreach, and blog content. |
| Yuval Gerber | Landing pages and analytics. |

The team reviews visits, signups, and useful feedback each week. Post frequency may change as the team learns which channels reach relevant groups.

## Communities

| Community | Audience and approach |
|---|---|
| [r/FFCommish](https://www.reddit.com/r/FFCommish/) | Fantasy league organizers discuss clear rules and group records. Participate in relevant discussions without promoting CallIt or adding product links. |
| [r/SideProject](https://www.reddit.com/r/SideProject/) | Builders discuss new products and can offer product feedback. Earlier CallIt posts were removed. Follow the community rules before posting again. |
| Columbia friend groups | Existing groups are the closest match to CallIt's audience. Share a short invitation only in groups where the sender is a member and the message is welcome. |

Community size and post reach vary. Use each platform's displayed reach where available and count website visits with tagged links. Do not treat overlapping audiences as unique people.

## Blog plan

A planned article, "Season-long calls: how to write a prediction your group can settle in April," explains how to write clear terms for a long-running prediction. It links to the [Agreements page](https://call-it.app/for/agreements?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=owned&utm_campaign=launch&utm_content=season_calls).

The article covers the question, choices, closing time, result definition, and a worked example. A second article can explore what a group prediction record should keep. Each published article will use a lowercase tagged link.

## Email and measurement

- Send the existing welcome email after a waitlist signup.
- Send an update or pilot invitation when there is useful news and the recipient has agreed to receive it.
- Use Google Analytics to compare visits and signups by page and tagged source.
- Use lowercase UTM values on campaign links.
- Review traffic and feedback weekly. Treat reach and signup forecasts as estimates until measured.

## Product pages
