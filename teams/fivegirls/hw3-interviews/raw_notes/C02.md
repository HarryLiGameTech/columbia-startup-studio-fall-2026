Interviewer reflections

Interpretation and open questions

The participant clearly described restroom cleanliness as affecting actual behavior. She abandoned a restroom because of its condition and spent additional time walking to another location.

Her current solution is mainly to rely on familiar stores and Google Maps, even though Google Maps does not provide the restroom-specific information she wants.

One important finding is that she does not require a restroom to be “perfectly clean.” She mainly wants to know whether it is usable. This could mean the product should emphasize concrete conditions such as wet floors, overflowing trash, smell, toilet paper availability, or whether stalls are usable rather than using a vague cleanliness score.

Another important issue is recency. The participant believes restroom conditions can change significantly during the same day, so old reviews may not be useful.

Open question: would enough users actually submit short cleanliness updates, given that this participant has never previously reported restroom conditions?
