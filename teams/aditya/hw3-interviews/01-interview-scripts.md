# HW3 — Interview scripts

These are the two scripts from the team’s interview kit. The notes do not establish that every question was asked in every interview.


## Idea 1: Group chat bot


### Part 1. Opening (2 min). Do not pitch.

"Hi, we're Lookman, Michelle, and Sam. We're working on a class project at Columbia, exploring how friend groups make plans together. We're not selling anything. We just want to understand your experience. Would you mind chatting for about 25 minutes?"

"By the way, it's OK to be brutally honest with me. You can't hurt my feelings. That's how we improve our ideas."

### Part 2. Who are they? (2 to 3 min)

- What year are you, and what do you study?

- How many active group chats are you in? Which ones do you use most?

- How often does your friend group try to make plans together?

### Part 3. Problem exploration (5 to 7 min)

- What is the hardest part about making plans with your friends?

- Can you tell me about the last time your group tried to plan something, like dinner or a night out?

- Why was that hard?

- How long did it take from "we should do something" to an actual plan?

- Who usually ends up organizing? How do they feel about it?

- Tell me about the last time plans fell through in the group chat. What happened?

- How do you pick where to eat when it's a group?

Keep asking why. Listen for sighs, eye rolls, and rants.

### Part 4. Current solutions (3 to 4 min)

- What, if anything, have you done to make planning easier? (for example When2Meet, polls, Google Maps, Resy, OpenTable, Partiful, Venmo)

- What don't you love about the tools you've tried?

- Money question: Have you ever spent money trying to solve this? How much? (for example a reservation fee, a paid app, a deposit you lost)

### Part 5. Solution signal (3 to 4 min). Now pitch briefly.

"So based on what you've told me, we're exploring building an AI that lives in your group chat. You can ask it to find a place to eat, check when everyone is free, and book it for the group."

- Have you tried anything like this before? If yes, why did you stop?

- What is your initial reaction to that?

- Would this solve the problem we were talking about?

- What concerns do you have with this? (privacy, an AI reading the chat, friends not using it)

### Part 6. Wrap-up (2 min)

- If you could wave a magic wand and change one thing about planning with friends, what would it be?

- Is there anything about making plans that I should have asked but didn't?

- Do you know 1 or 2 other people who deal with this? Would you connect me?

- Would you be open to a 10-minute follow-up in a couple of weeks?

- If you could pre-purchase this right now for $10, would you?

## Idea 2: Subleasing and renting


### Part 1. Opening (2 min). Do not pitch.

"Hi, we're Lookman, Michelle, and Sam. We're working on a class project at Columbia, exploring how students find places to live and sublease. We're not selling anything. We just want to understand your experience. Would you mind chatting for about 25 minutes?"

"By the way, it's OK to be brutally honest with me. You can't hurt my feelings. That's how we improve our ideas."

### Part 2. Who are they? (2 to 3 min)

- What year are you, and what do you study?

- Where do you live now? Campus housing, off campus, a sublet?

- Have you ever subleased your place, or rented a sublet from someone?

### Part 3. Problem exploration (5 to 7 min)

- What is the hardest part about finding a place to live, or finding someone to take your place?

- Can you tell me about the last time you looked for a sublet or apartment?

- Why was that hard?

- Where did you look? How many places or sites did you check?

- How long did the whole process take?

- Tell me about a time you worried a listing or a person was fake or a scam.

- How did you check that the person was really a student?

Keep asking why. Listen for stress, deadlines, and stories about scams.

### Part 4. Current solutions (3 to 4 min)

- What, if anything, have you done to find a place or a subletter? (for example Facebook groups, Columbia housing pages, Listings Project, StreetEasy, GroupMe, friends)

- What don't you love about the solutions you've tried?

- Money question: Have you ever spent money trying to solve this? How much? (for example a broker fee, an application fee, rent you paid on an empty room)

### Part 5. Solution signal (3 to 4 min). Now pitch briefly.

"So based on what you've told me, we're exploring building one place for students to sublease and rent. Everyone is verified with a school email, and the listing, chat, and agreement all live in one spot."

- Have you tried anything like this before? If yes, why did you stop?

- What is your initial reaction to that?

- Would this solve the problem we were talking about?

- What concerns do you have with this?

### Part 6. Wrap-up (2 min)

- If you could wave a magic wand and change one thing about subleasing or renting, what would it be?

- Is there anything about finding a place that I should have asked but didn't?

- Do you know 1 or 2 other people who deal with this? Would you connect me?

- Would you be open to a 10-minute follow-up in a couple of weeks?

- If you could pre-purchase this right now for $10, would you?

Class rule reminder for Idea 2: do not give legal or financial advice about leases, and do not collect government IDs. Verify with a school email only.
