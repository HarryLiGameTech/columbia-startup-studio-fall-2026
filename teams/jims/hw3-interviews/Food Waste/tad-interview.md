# Interview Notes: Tad (Campus Food Waste)

**Interviewed:** October 2, 2026

## Raw interview notes

- Tad is a Columbia graduate student living with his wife, who is also a graduate student.
- He and his wife cook nearly every day. Lately he has tried Columbia Dining, and they rarely order food because it is expensive.
- Grocery shopping has been difficult because neighborhood groceries are expensive.
- TA meetings and seminars usually provide food.
- Free food would draw him to an event: "Yes, 100%." and "Yeah, if there's free food, uh, for sure."
- He would like a short description of the food beforehand.
- Healthy food is not required: he would still attend for pizza or other less-healthy food.
- He would travel anywhere on campus or up to about 10 blocks for a formal meal, but only a couple of blocks for pizza leftovers.
