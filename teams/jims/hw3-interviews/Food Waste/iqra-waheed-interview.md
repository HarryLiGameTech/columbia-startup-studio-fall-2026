# Interview Notes: Iqra Waheed (Campus Food Waste)

## What I learned

- **Who:** Former class president and current student involved in several campus organizations and events. She has organized events and ordered food for groups.
- **Problem confirmed?:** Yes, very strongly. Iqra said she has leftover food "every time," usually enough for at least 10 additional people. At a recent event with 15 attendees, enough food remained for another 15.
- **Key quote:** "I can't tell you one event where food adequately ran out."
- **Emotional intensity:** 9/10. She used strong language about the frequency of the problem and gave several examples of personally distributing leftovers.

## Solutions and reaction

- **Current solution:** Gives food to attendees, asks them to take it home, offers individual items such as brownies, contacts friends, or finds a place to leave larger trays. At a recent event, she and another student carried leftover trays to Barnard campus security because other places were closed.
- **Money spent?:** No evidence she personally pays to solve leftovers, though event budgets pay for catering. Barnard catering is cheaper but produces more excess food; outside restaurants are pricier, making her order more carefully.
- **Solution reaction:** Very positive toward a fast notification system. She wanted interested students to sign up so an organizer could quickly share where food is available. First come, first served was acceptable. She also liked a simple preset post including capacity, dietary restrictions, and location.
- **Biggest concern:** Effort and irrelevant notifications. She did not want to alert the entire student body or create an Instagram-style post. A short text, SideChat-style message, or email would be fine. She questioned whether students would download another app when SideChat might already work.

## Commitments and signal

- **Referral?:** Yes. She immediately agreed to refer another experienced event organizer and began looking for their contact information.
- **Follow-up?:** Yes. She agreed to meet again and beta-test a solution.
- **Surprise:** Waste was consistent, not occasional. Even an event with the expected 15 attendees produced enough leftovers for about 15 more people. The problem is also timing and distribution: events end around 8-9 PM, designated sites may be closed, and students do not want to carry large trays across campus.
- **Signal:** Green. The interview strongly validates the organizer problem, a lightweight notification solution, and willingness to support testing. The system must be exceptionally quick and avoid irrelevant mass notifications or complicated posts.
