# Campus Food Waste Interview Guide

## Goal

Validate whether campus food waste after club events is a real, frequent problem and whether students need access to leftover food.

## Introduction

> We are doing research about food at campus events and how students access food on campus. We are not testing you, and there are no right or wrong answers. We are interested in hearing about your actual experiences.

## Questions for club leaders and event organizers

1. Tell me about the last event your club hosted where you provided food.
2. How do you decide how much food to order?
3. Was there any food left over? About how much?
4. What did you do with the leftover food?
5. How often does your club end up with leftover food?
6. What usually prevents you from giving the leftovers to other students?
7. Have you ever tried to tell other students that leftover food was available? How did you do it?
8. What was difficult or inconvenient about that process?
9. How quickly do you normally need to get rid of the food after an event?
10. If there were a very quick way to notify students about leftover food, what concerns or problems would you have with using it?

## Questions for students

1. Tell me about how you normally get your meals on campus.
2. Have you ever looked for free food from campus events?
3. Tell me about the last time you got free or leftover food from an event.
4. How did you find out that the food was available?
5. Have there been times when you found out about free food too late?
6. Where do you currently hear about free food: group chats, Instagram, friends, email, or elsewhere?
7. What is frustrating about the current way you find out about it?
8. If you heard leftover food was available somewhere on campus, what information would you need before deciding to go?
9. How far would you realistically walk for leftover food?
10. How quickly could you usually pick it up after receiving a notification?

## Research questions to answer

1. Is there actually enough leftover food?
2. Is getting rid of leftovers painful enough for organizers to act?
3. Is there real student demand?
4. Can the timing work if food needs to disappear within 20-60 minutes?

## Interview snapshot template

Fill this out within one hour of each interview.

### What you learned

- **Who:** Name or description
- **Problem confirmed?:** Did they describe it unprompted?
- **Key quote:** Most revealing statement, in exact words
- **Emotional intensity:** 1-10

### Solutions and reaction

- **Current solution:** What are they doing now?
- **Money spent?:** Have they paid for anything related?
- **Solution reaction:** What did they say or do?
- **Biggest concern:** What made them hesitate?

### Commitments and signal

- **Referral?:** Did they offer to connect you with others?
- **Follow-up?:** Did they agree to talk again?
- **Surprise:** What contradicted assumptions?
- **Signal:** Green, yellow, or red
