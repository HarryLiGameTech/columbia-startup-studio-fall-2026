# Style Guide: AstraPath

## 1. Visual Identity & Mood
AstraPath's visual identity communicates clarity, precision, and dependability in dark or low-connectivity spaces. The look balances modern technical sophistication with high contrast and legibility, ensuring the app is effortless to read on a phone screen while walking through dimly lit or crowded venues.

- **Mood:** Navigational, crisp, reliable, futuristic yet accessible.
- **Design References:** Flight radar interfaces, modern star-chart applications, minimalist transit maps (e.g., Transit, Apple Maps night mode).

---

## 2. Color Palette

### Primary Colors
- **Deep Navy (`#0A192F` / `#0D1117`):** Represents background environments, low-light spaces, and offline reliability.
- **Constellation Blue (`#4A90E2` / `#58A6FF`):** Core brand color used for navigation paths, structural UI nodes, and interactive elements.

### Accent & Status Colors
- **Beacon Yellow (`#FFD166` / `#F2C94C`):** Used for primary target markers, stars, focal destination points, and key call-to-action buttons.
- **Signal White (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary text and key node highlights for maximum contrast against dark backgrounds.
- **Muted Starlight Gray (`#8B949E`):** Secondary text, background stars, and inactive path lines.

---

## 3. Typography

- **Primary Headings (Display & UI Headers):** Inter or SF Pro Display (Bold / Semi-Bold)
  - *Usage:* Screen titles, clear direction prompts (e.g., "Turn Left in 20 ft"), major navigation headers.
- **Body Text & Interface:** SF Pro Text or System Sans-Serif (Regular / Medium)
  - *Usage:* Conversational agent feedback, instructions, menu options, button labels.
- **Monospace / Technical Data:** JetBrains Mono or SF Mono
  - *Usage:* Offline signal indicators, step counters, precise distance metrics.

---

## 4. Logo Guidelines

### Mark & Name Lockup Rules
- **Horizontal Lockup:** The brand mark sits directly to the left of the "AstraPath" wordmark.
- **Proportions:** The mark's overall height matches the cap-height of the wordmark's capital letters.
- **Stacked Version:** Used for centered layouts or vertical constraints; the mark sits directly above the "AstraPath" wordmark.

### Color & Background Usage
- **Light Backgrounds:** Full-color mark paired with Deep Navy (`#0A192F`) text.
- **Dark Backgrounds:** Full-color mark paired with Signal White (`#FFFFFF`) text on Deep Navy.
- **Monochrome:** Single-color production needs use solid black on white backgrounds or solid white on dark backgrounds.

### Minimum Sizes & Favicons
- **Minimum Operating Size:** The standalone mark should not be rendered smaller than 16x16 px for icon use or 24 px tall in full horizontal lockup.
- **Favicon & Avatar Asset Set:** Exported at standard web/mobile sizes including 16, 32, 48, 180, 192, and 512 pixels.
