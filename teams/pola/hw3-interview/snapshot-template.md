# Interview snapshot template

Copy this for each actual interview and complete it within one hour. This is a blank template, not an interview record. The three sections and 12 fields follow class 3 PDF pages 67-69; the metadata and bracketed prompts are team additions. Use participant wording for quotes and label your own interpretations.

**Interviewer:** [Name]  
**Interview ended:** [YYYY-MM-DD, time, time zone]  
**Snapshot completed:** [YYYY-MM-DD, time, time zone]

## Interview snapshot: what you learned

| Field | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Who | [Name or anonymous description; relevant persona and recent experience.] |
| Problem confirmed? | [Yes / Partly / No / Unclear. Did they describe the specific problem before you introduced it? Identify the question or moment; separate unprompted description from agreement after prompting.] |
| Key quote | [Exact words of the most revealing statement. If no verbatim quote was captured, say so; do not reconstruct one.] |
| Emotional intensity | [1-10; interviewer estimate with observed words or behavior supporting it, or Not observed.] |

## Interview snapshot: solutions and reaction

| Field | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Current solution | [What they actually do now; a recent example and what works or fails.] |
| Money spent? | [Actual past amount, currency, purchase, and connection to this problem; $0, Unknown, or Not asked as appropriate.] |
| Solution reaction | [Initial exact words and observable behavior after the concept was introduced. If omitted, Not presented.] |
| Biggest concern | [What made them hesitate, preferably in their words; None stated or Not asked if applicable.] |

## Interview snapshot: commitments and signal

| Field | What to write |
| --- | --- |
| Referral? | [Offered / Asked and agreed / Declined / Not asked; introduction completed or pending.] |
| Follow-up? | [Agreed / Declined / Not asked; scheduled time or Not scheduled.] |
| Surprise | [What contradicted the team's assumption; distinguish evidence from your interpretation.] |
| Signal | [Green / Yellow / Red, with evidence and limitations. Use the working convention in README.md.] |
