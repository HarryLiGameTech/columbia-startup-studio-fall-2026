# Campus departure interview script

**Idea #6:** Predictive Campus Departure Assistant. **Target:** Five students who move between campus buildings for classes, with a mix of new and experienced students.

**Length: 5-10 minutes.** The six core sections total about five minutes. Follow only the applicable branch; use at most two optional follow-ups if they have more time. Let them talk; skip questions already answered. Do not introduce the concept before section 5.

## 1 Opening

**15 seconds.**

> Hi, I'm [name], doing a Columbia class project on getting between classes. Do you have five minutes, and is it okay if I take notes? Honest answers help, including if this isn't a problem for you.

## 2 Context

**30 seconds.**

> What was your most recent trip between classes like?

## 3 Recent experience

**2 minutes.**

> What's the hardest part, if anything, about getting to your next class?

- **If they describe a difficulty:** “Walk me through the last time. How did you decide when to leave, and what happened?” Then: “What did that cost you in time or consequences, and how often has it happened in the past two weeks?”
- **If it works fine:** “What makes your routine work? When did it last go wrong, if ever?” Accept no problem as an answer and move on.

Do not suggest lateness, elevators, or stronger reminders before hearing their account. Distinguish uncertain travel time from being unable to leave a previous class.

## 4 Current solution

**45 seconds.**

> What do you currently do to handle this, and how well does it work? Have you spent money specifically on it? How much?

Count actual spending on the problem, not a phone or watch they already owned.

## 5 Concept reaction

**1 minute.**

> We're exploring a phone or watch assistant that uses your schedule and current location to estimate the trip to your classroom and suggest when to leave. What's your first reaction? What's your biggest concern?

Capture their first words. If they had no difficulty, do not treat polite interest as confirmation of a problem.

## 6 Close

**30 seconds.**

> Could you introduce us to someone with a similar experience? May we contact you for a brief follow-up?

Thank them. Record an introduction or scheduled time only if it actually happens.

## Optional follow-ups

Use at most two, only if they agree to continue beyond five minutes. Stop by ten minutes.

- **Unclear cause:** “Where did the trip differ from what you expected?”
- **Existing workaround:** “Tell me about the last time that approach failed.”
- **Concept concern:** “Have you tried something similar? What happened?” For a permission or alert concern, ask for their last experience with that issue.
